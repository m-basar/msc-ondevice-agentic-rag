# Expenses Policy

> This version has been withdrawn. See HR-13 for the policy in force.

## Mileage

Employees using a private vehicle for business travel may claim 40 pence per mile. Journeys between home and the normal place of work are not claimable.

## Meals

| Circumstance | Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakfast, where travel begins before 06:00 | £6 |
| Lunch, where away from site for more than 5 hours | £8 |
| Evening meal, where returning after 20:00 | £18 |

Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at £110 per night outside London and £140 per night within the M25. Bookings must be made through the nominated travel agent wherever possible.

## Submission

Claims are submitted on the paper expenses form with original receipts attached, and must reach Finance within 60 days of the expenditure. Claims are reimbursed with the following month's salary.
