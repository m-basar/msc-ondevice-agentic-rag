# Refunds Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope

This procedure covers refunding money to customers. The physical handling and inspection of returned goods is covered in OPS-02. A refund is not processed until the disposition in OPS-02 has been recorded.

## Timing

| Customer type | Refund issued within |
|---|---|
| Consumer, statutory cancellation | 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of the customer supplying evidence that they have sent them, whichever is earlier |
| Consumer, standard 30-day return | 10 working days of disposition |
| Trade account | Credit note within 5 working days of disposition |

## Method

Refunds are made to the original payment method. Card refunds are processed by Finance through the payment gateway; the funds typically reach the customer's account three to five working days later, which is outside the company's control.

Trade customers are refunded by credit note against their account, not by bank transfer, unless the account is closed.

Refunds are never issued in cash, and never through petty cash.

## Amount

The refund covers the price paid for the goods. Original outbound delivery is refunded where the customer is exercising a statutory cancellation right or where the goods were faulty, and is not refunded for a discretionary change-of-mind return.

Where disposition B has been recorded, a restocking deduction of up to 15% may be applied at the discretion of the Customer Services Manager, and the customer must be told before the deduction is made.

## Authorisation

| Refund value | Authorised by |
|---|---|
| Up to £250 | Customer Services advisor |
| £251 to £1,000 | Customer Services Manager |
| Above £1,000 | Finance Manager |
