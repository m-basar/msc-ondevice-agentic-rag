# Expenses Policy

This policy replaces HR-03 with effect from 1 April 2026.

## Mileage

Employees using a private vehicle for business travel may claim 55 pence per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year, and 25 pence per mile thereafter. These rates match the HMRC approved mileage allowance payment rates that took effect on 6 April 2026, so no tax liability arises and no Mileage Allowance Relief is due.

Journeys between home and the normal place of work are not claimable. Where an employee travels from home directly to a customer site, the claimable distance is the lesser of the actual journey and the journey from the office.

## Meals

| Circumstance | Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakfast, where travel begins before 06:00 | £8 |
| Lunch, where away from site for more than 5 hours | £10 |
| Evening meal, where returning after 20:00 | £22 |

Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at £130 per night outside London and £170 per night within the M25. Bookings must be made through the company travel portal. Where the cap cannot be met, prior approval from the department head is required.

## Submission

Claims are submitted through the expenses module of the People system with photographed receipts attached. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expenditure. Claims older than 90 days will not be paid.

Approved claims are reimbursed by BACS within ten working days of approval, no longer with salary.
