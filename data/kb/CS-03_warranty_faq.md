# Warranty Frequently Asked Questions

## How long do I have to return an item?

Unwanted items may be returned within 28 days of delivery provided they are unused and in their original packaging. This is separate from a warranty claim, which may be made at any point during the warranty period.

## What warranty do you offer?

| Use | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Domestic use | 2 years from date of delivery |
| Commercial or catering use | 1 year from date of delivery |

Some manufacturers offer longer cover on specific ranges, stated on the product page. Where the manufacturer's warranty is longer, it applies.

The warranty is in addition to statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which are not affected by it.

## What does the warranty cover?

Faults arising from defective materials or manufacture in normal use.

## What is not covered?

- Normal wear, including blade dulling, non-stick coating wear and handle discolouration
- Damage from misuse, dropping, or overheating an empty pan
- Dishwasher damage to items marked hand wash only
- Commercial use of items sold as domestic
- Consumable parts such as blender seals and filters
- Rust on carbon steel or cast iron, which is a maintenance matter covered in PRD-02

## How do I claim?

Contact customer services with the order number, a description of the fault, and photographs. Where the fault is not obvious from photographs, the item is returned under a Returns Authorisation number and inspected.

Assessment normally takes five working days from receipt.

## What do I get if the claim succeeds?

Repair, replacement, or refund, in that order of preference, at the company's discretion having regard to the customer's statutory rights. Where an item is out of stock or discontinued, a replacement of equivalent specification or a refund is offered.

## Do I need the receipt?

The order number is sufficient for anything bought directly from us. Items bought through a third-party retailer are claimed through that retailer.
