# Data Protection and Information Handling

## Legal basis

Northgate Kitchenware Ltd processes personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The company is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. The Data Protection Lead is the Finance Manager, contactable at dpo@northgate-kitchenware.invalid.

## Classification

| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Catalogue, published prices | No restriction |
| Internal | Procedures, stock levels | Company systems only |
| Confidential | Customer orders, supplier terms | Company systems, access on need-to-know |
| Sensitive | Payroll, health, disciplinary records | Named individuals only, encrypted at rest |

## Handling rules

Personal data is stored only in approved company systems. It must not be copied to personal email, personal cloud storage, USB drives, or personal devices.

Customer card details are never recorded in the order system, in email, or on paper. Card payments are taken only through the payment gateway.

Emails containing confidential or sensitive data sent outside the company must be encrypted or sent through the secure portal. Check the recipient address before sending; misdirected email is the most common cause of data breaches.

Paper records containing personal data are locked away when unattended and shredded when no longer needed, never placed in general waste.

## Access review

Access to systems holding personal data is reviewed annually by the Data Protection Lead. Managers confirm in writing that every account in their area is still required.

## Individual rights

Requests from individuals to access, correct or erase their data are forwarded to the Data Protection Lead on the day received. The company must respond within one calendar month, so delay in forwarding a request creates real risk.

## Suppliers

Any supplier processing personal data on the company's behalf must have a written data processing agreement in place before processing begins.
