# Procurement Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all purchases of goods and services other than stock for resale, which is bought against supplier agreements by the buying team.

## Purchase orders

No commitment may be made to a supplier without an approved purchase order. Suppliers are told that invoices without a valid PO number will not be paid. A retrospective PO raised after the commitment is a policy breach and is reported to the Finance Manager.

## Authority limits

| Value, excluding VAT | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to £500 | Line manager |
| £501 to £2,500 | Department head |
| £2,501 to £10,000 | Finance Manager |
| Above £10,000 | Managing Director |
| Above £50,000 | Managing Director and one other director |

## Competitive quotations

| Value, excluding VAT | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Up to £2,500 | One quotation |
| £2,501 to £10,000 | Three written quotations |
| Above £10,000 | Three written quotations and a documented evaluation |

Where three quotations cannot reasonably be obtained, for example where only one supplier can provide the item, a single-source justification is written and approved by the Finance Manager before commitment.

Award is made on value for money, not lowest price alone. The evaluation records why the chosen supplier was selected.

## Conflicts of interest

Any personal, family or financial relationship with a supplier or potential supplier must be declared in writing to the Finance Manager. The individual then takes no part in the selection decision.

## New suppliers

New suppliers are set up by Finance only, on receipt of a completed supplier form with bank details confirmed by telephone to a number obtained independently, not from the form. This control exists to prevent invoice redirection fraud.
