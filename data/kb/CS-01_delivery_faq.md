# Delivery Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 working days from despatch. Next working day delivery is available on orders placed and paid before 14:00, Monday to Thursday. Orders placed after 14:00 on Thursday for next-day delivery arrive on Monday, as the carrier does not deliver at weekends.

## What does delivery cost?

| Service | Cost, excluding VAT |
|---|---|
| Standard, orders under £75 | £6.95 |
| Standard, orders £75 and over | Free |
| Next working day | £12.95 |
| Pre-noon next working day | £19.95 |
| Highlands, islands and Northern Ireland | £14.95, 3 to 7 working days |

Free delivery applies to the goods value excluding VAT and excluding any surcharge postcodes.

## Where do you deliver?

Mainland Great Britain, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands. The company does not currently ship outside the United Kingdom.

## Can I collect my order?

Yes. Select collection at checkout and collect from the trade counter, open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 17:00. Orders are normally ready within two hours of confirmation. Bring the order number and photographic identification.

## What if I am not in?

The carrier attempts delivery once, then leaves a card and takes the parcel to a local collection point where it is held for seven days. Parcels not collected are returned to us and the order refunded less outbound carriage.

## Do you deliver on Saturdays?

Saturday delivery is not offered. The warehouse is open Saturday mornings for collections only.

## My order has not arrived

Check the tracking link in your despatch email first. If tracking shows no movement for two working days, contact customer services on 024 7496 3300 with your order number.
