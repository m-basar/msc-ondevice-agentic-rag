# Forklift Truck Operations

## Authorised operators

Only employees holding a current RTITB or equivalent accredited certificate for the relevant truck category, and who appear on the site authorised operator list, may operate a forklift. Authorisation is site-specific and issued in writing by the Warehouse Manager. Refresher training is required every three years.

The company operates three counterbalance trucks and two reach trucks. Authorisation is per category; a counterbalance certificate does not authorise reach truck operation.

## Pre-use inspection

A documented pre-use check is completed at the start of every shift, before the truck is used, covering:

- Tyres, forks and mast for damage
- Hydraulic leaks
- Brakes, including the parking brake
- Horn, lights and beacon
- Battery charge and electrolyte level where applicable
- Seat belt condition and operation

Defects are recorded on the daily check sheet. A truck with a safety-critical defect is taken out of service, the key removed, and a "do not use" tag applied. Report to the Warehouse Supervisor immediately.

## Operating rules

- The seat belt must be worn at all times while the truck is in motion
- Maximum speed inside the building is 4 km/h
- Travel with the forks lowered approximately 100 mm from the floor and the mast tilted back
- Travel in reverse when the load obstructs forward vision
- Never lift or carry a passenger
- Never leave a truck unattended with the forks raised or the engine running
- Sound the horn at aisle ends, blind corners and the goods-in door

## End of shift

Park in the designated bay, lower the forks fully, apply the parking brake, switch off, remove the key and return it to the key cabinet. Trucks on charge must have the charging area kept clear and ventilated.

## Statutory inspection

Every truck receives a thorough examination every 12 months under LOLER 1998. Certificates are held in the warehouse office.
