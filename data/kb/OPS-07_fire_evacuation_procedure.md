# Fire Evacuation Procedure

## On discovering a fire

1. Raise the alarm by breaking the nearest call point
2. Telephone 999 from a safe location, or ask a colleague to do so
3. Leave by the nearest available exit
4. Do not attempt to tackle the fire unless you are trained, the fire is small, and your escape route is clear behind you

## On hearing the alarm

The alarm is a continuous siren. There is no two-stage alarm on this site; a continuous siren means evacuate immediately.

Leave the building by the nearest available fire exit. Do not stop to collect personal belongings. Do not use the lift. Close doors behind you where you can do so without delay.

Forklift operators lower the forks, apply the parking brake, switch off, and leave the truck where it stands.

## Assembly point

The assembly point is the north corner of the staff car park, beside the blue container.

Do not cross the yard through the goods-in bay. Use the marked pedestrian route around the outside of the building.

Do not re-enter the building for any reason until a fire warden confirms it is safe.

## Roll call

Fire wardens take a roll call at the assembly point using the daily attendance list and the visitor book from reception. Managers must account for their team and report to the senior warden.

Anyone who has left site during the day but not signed out will be reported as unaccounted for, so signing out matters.

## Wardens and equipment

The site has six trained fire wardens, identified by yellow high-visibility tabards kept at each fire point. Warden training is refreshed every two years.

Fire extinguishers are inspected annually by a competent contractor. The alarm is tested every Wednesday at 10:00; this is a short test and does not require evacuation.

A full evacuation drill is held twice per year.
