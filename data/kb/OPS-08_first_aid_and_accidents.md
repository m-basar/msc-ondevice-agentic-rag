# First Aid and Accident Reporting

## First aiders

The site has four qualified first aiders. Their names and extension numbers are displayed at reception, in the warehouse office, and by the staff kitchen. Certification is renewed every three years.

In a medical emergency, call 999 first, then contact a first aider on extension 4099.

## First aid provision

First aid kits are located at reception, the warehouse office, the goods-in desk, and the trade counter. An eyewash station is fitted next to the cleaning store. Kit contents are checked monthly by the Health and Safety Coordinator.

There is a defibrillator mounted in the reception corridor. It gives spoken instructions and may be used by anyone.

## Reporting

Every accident, injury and near miss must be recorded in the accident book held in the warehouse office, on the same day wherever possible. This includes incidents involving visitors and contractors, and incidents where no injury resulted.

Near misses are reported because they identify hazards before someone is hurt. There is no blame attached to reporting one.

## Escalation

The Health and Safety Coordinator reviews every entry within two working days and decides whether it is reportable under RIDDOR 2013.

| Category | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Death or specified injury | Report to HSE without delay, then within 10 days |
| Over-seven-day incapacitation | Report within 15 days |
| Dangerous occurrence | Report without delay, then within 10 days |
| Occupational disease | Report on diagnosis |

## Investigation

Any accident causing injury, and any near miss with the potential to cause serious injury, is investigated by the Health and Safety Coordinator with the relevant supervisor. Findings and corrective actions are reported to the senior team monthly.
