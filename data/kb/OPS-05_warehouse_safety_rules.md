# Warehouse Safety Rules

## Personal protective equipment

Safety footwear with toe protection is mandatory for everyone in the warehouse at all times, including visitors and managers passing through. High-visibility clothing is mandatory in all areas where forklifts operate. Cut-resistant gloves must be worn when handling unboxed knives or broken glassware.

## Pedestrian routes

Pedestrians must keep to the green marked walkways. Where a walkway crosses a forklift aisle, pedestrians stop, make eye contact with the driver, and wait to be waved across. Never assume a driver has seen you.

## Manual handling

The single-person lift limit is 20 kg. Loads above this require a second person or mechanical assistance. Trolleys and pallet trucks are available at each aisle end.

Assess the load before lifting: check the weight marking, test the load, keep it close to the body, bend the knees rather than the back, and avoid twisting.

## Racking

Do not climb racking under any circumstances. Use the designated step ladders for the lower two levels and request a forklift with a cage for anything higher.

Report damaged racking uprights or beams to the Warehouse Supervisor immediately and cordon off the bay. Damaged racking is not used until inspected.

## Housekeeping

Aisles and fire exits are kept clear at all times. Spillages are cleaned immediately and a wet floor sign placed. Broken pallets go to the designated skip, not against a wall.

## Speed and vehicles

The site speed limit is 8 km/h in the yard and 4 km/h inside the building. Reversing into a bay requires a banksman where the driver's view is obstructed.

## Stopping work

Any employee may stop a task they believe to be unsafe without fear of consequence. Report the concern to the supervisor immediately.
