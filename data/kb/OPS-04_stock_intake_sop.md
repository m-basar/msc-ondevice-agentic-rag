# Stock Intake Standard Operating Procedure

## Booking in

Suppliers must book a delivery slot in advance through the goods-in email address. Deliveries arriving without a booked slot are accepted only if a bay is free, and may be turned away during peak periods.

Delivery slots run from 07:30 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. No inbound deliveries are accepted on Saturdays.

## Receipt check

On arrival the goods-in operative:

1. Checks the delivery note against the purchase order number
2. Counts pallets or cartons against the delivery note
3. Inspects for visible external damage before the driver leaves
4. Signs the driver's paperwork, noting any discrepancy or damage in writing

Signing "unchecked" is not permitted. Where damage is visible, the operative writes "damaged on arrival" on the driver's copy and photographs the pallet before it is moved.

## Detailed check

Cartons are opened and item quantities verified within one working day of receipt. Discrepancies are recorded on the system and reported to Procurement the same day so a claim can be raised within the supplier's notification window, usually seven days.

## Putaway

Stock is booked onto the system before putaway, never after. Each item is placed in its designated location. Where no location exists, the operative requests one from the Warehouse Supervisor rather than choosing an empty space.

Knives and other bladed goods are stored in the secure cage, not on open racking.

## Stock counts

A perpetual inventory count covers the whole warehouse over each quarter, with high-value lines counted monthly. A full wall-to-wall count is performed once a year at the end of March.
