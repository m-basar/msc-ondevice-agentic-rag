# Returns Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope

This procedure covers the physical handling of returned goods: authorisation, receipt, inspection and disposition. The financial side, including when money is refunded and by what method, is covered separately in OPS-03.

## Return authorisation

No goods are accepted back without a Returns Authorisation number. Customer Services issues an RA number in the format RA-NNNNN, valid for 21 days.

Goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in their original packaging. Consumer customers additionally have the statutory 14-day cancellation right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, which runs from the day after delivery and is not affected by the 30-day policy.

The following are not accepted for return unless faulty: bespoke engraved items, sharpened knives, and any item with broken hygiene seals.

## Receipt

Returned parcels are booked in at the goods-in desk against the RA number on the same day they arrive. Parcels without a visible RA number are quarantined in the returns cage and Customer Services is notified.

## Inspection

A returns operative inspects each item within two working days of receipt and assigns a disposition:

| Disposition | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A | As new, resaleable | Return to stock |
| B | Opened, undamaged | Return to stock, flag for discount channel |
| C | Damaged or used | Do not restock, photograph, quarantine |
| D | Faulty within warranty | Photograph, route to supplier claim under CS-03 |

Every disposition is recorded against the RA number with the operative's initials.

## Carriage

Where the return is due to company error or a faulty item, the company pays return carriage. Otherwise the customer pays.
