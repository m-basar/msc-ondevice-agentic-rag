# Petty Cash Procedure

## Purpose

Petty cash exists for small, unforeseen operational purchases where raising a purchase order is disproportionate. It is not an alternative to the procurement process in FIN-03, and it is not a means of reimbursing employee expenses, which are claimed under HR-13.

## Float

The float is £250, held in the locked cash box in the Finance office. The Finance Assistant is the custodian. A second key is held by the Finance Manager.

## Limits

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Maximum single transaction | £50 |
| Maximum per person per week | £100 |
| Items requiring Finance Manager approval | Any purchase above £25 |

Splitting a purchase into smaller amounts to stay under a limit is a disciplinary matter.

## What may be purchased

Permitted: postage, small consumables, refreshments for customer meetings, emergency stationery, taxi fares in operational emergencies.

Not permitted: alcohol, gift cards, personal items, IT equipment or software of any value, anything available through a standard supplier account, and anything above £50.

## Process

1. Obtain approval from the Finance Manager where the amount exceeds £25, before the purchase
2. Purchase the item and obtain a VAT receipt
3. Complete a petty cash voucher with date, amount, purpose and cost centre
4. Present the voucher and receipt to the Finance Assistant
5. Sign the petty cash book on receiving the cash

Claims without a receipt are not reimbursed. A missing receipt declaration may be accepted once per person per year, at the Finance Manager's discretion.

## Reconciliation

The Finance Assistant reconciles the box weekly. Cash plus vouchers must equal £250. The Finance Manager performs an unannounced spot check each quarter and signs the book.
