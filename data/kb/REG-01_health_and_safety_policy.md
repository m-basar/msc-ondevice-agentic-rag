# Health and Safety Policy

## Statement of intent

Northgate Kitchenware Ltd is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for employees, contractors, visitors and anyone affected by its activities, so far as is reasonably practicable. This policy is reviewed annually and is signed by the Managing Director.

The company has more than five employees, so this policy is written down as required by the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Overall responsibility, provision of resources |
| Health and Safety Coordinator | Day-to-day management, risk assessments, training records, accident investigation |
| Department heads | Safety within their area, ensuring assessments are followed |
| Line managers | Briefing their teams, addressing unsafe practice |
| All employees | Taking reasonable care of themselves and others, following procedures, reporting hazards |

Employees have a legal duty to co-operate on health and safety matters and must not interfere with anything provided for safety.

## Risk assessment

Risk assessments are completed for all significant activities, reviewed annually, and reviewed immediately following an accident, a near miss, or a change to the activity. Assessments are held in the warehouse office and on the shared drive.

Specific assessments cover manual handling, workplace transport, working at height, display screen equipment, COSHH, and new and expectant mothers.

## Consultation

Safety matters are a standing item at the monthly team briefing. Employees may raise concerns directly with the Health and Safety Coordinator or anonymously through the suggestion box at reception.

## Key procedures

Fire evacuation is covered in OPS-07, first aid and accident reporting in OPS-08, warehouse rules in OPS-05, and forklift operation in OPS-06.

## Monitoring

The Health and Safety Coordinator conducts a documented site inspection monthly. Findings and outstanding actions are reported to the senior team. An external health and safety consultant audits the site annually.
