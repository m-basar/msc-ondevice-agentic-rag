# Security Incident Reporting

## What counts as an incident

Report any of the following:

- A lost or stolen laptop, phone or USB device
- An email sent to the wrong recipient containing personal or confidential data
- Clicking a link or opening an attachment in a suspected phishing email
- Suspected malware, ransomware or unexpected system behaviour
- Unauthorised access to a system, room or filing cabinet
- Paper records lost, or found where they should not be
- Any suspicion that a password has been compromised

If you are unsure whether something is an incident, report it. Reporting something harmless costs a few minutes; not reporting a real breach can cost the company a great deal more.

## How to report

Telephone the IT helpdesk on extension 4040 immediately, and follow up by email to ithelp@northgatekitchenware.co.uk. Do not attempt to fix or investigate the problem yourself, and do not delete anything, as evidence may be needed.

## Timescales

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Report to IT helpdesk | Within 1 hour of becoming aware |
| IT initial assessment | Within 4 hours |
| Notify Data Protection Lead if personal data is involved | Within 24 hours |
| Notify the ICO if the breach is likely to risk individuals' rights | Within 72 hours of the company becoming aware |
| Notify affected individuals if the risk is high | Without undue delay |

## No blame

Staff who report their own mistakes promptly and honestly will not face disciplinary action for the mistake itself. Concealing an incident, or failing to report one, may be treated as misconduct.

## Records

All incidents are recorded in the incident log, whether or not they are reportable to the ICO. The log is reviewed quarterly by the senior team.
