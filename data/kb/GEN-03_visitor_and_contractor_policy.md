# Visitor and Contractor Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how visitors and contractors are managed on site, so that the company meets its duties under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and can account for everyone present in the event of an evacuation.

## All visitors

Every visitor must sign in at reception on arrival and sign out on departure. Visitors are issued with a numbered badge, which must be worn visibly at all times and returned on departure.

Visitors must be accompanied by a member of staff at all times when outside the reception and meeting room areas. The member of staff who arranged the visit is responsible for the visitor's safety and for ensuring they evacuate if the alarm sounds.

Visitors are not permitted in the warehouse unless they have received the site safety briefing and are wearing high-visibility clothing and safety footwear. Both are available at the warehouse office.

## Contractors

Contractors must additionally:

- Provide a valid public liability insurance certificate before the first visit
- Complete a site induction with the Facilities Coordinator
- Submit a risk assessment and method statement for any work involving working at height, hot work, or electrical isolation
- Obtain a signed permit to work from the Facilities Coordinator before starting hot work or roof access

## Deliveries

Delivery drivers report to the goods-in door and remain in the marked waiting area. Drivers do not require a visitor badge but must not enter the racking aisles.
