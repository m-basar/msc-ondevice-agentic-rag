# Company Overview

Northgate Kitchenware Ltd is a wholesale distributor of professional and domestic kitchen equipment. The company was founded in 2011 and employs 47 staff.

Northgate Kitchenware Ltd is a fictional organisation created for research purposes. It is not registered at Companies House, and no company registration number is quoted anywhere in this knowledge base. All telephone numbers use the Ofcom range reserved for drama, all email addresses use the reserved .invalid domain, and the postcode is deliberately outside any allocated postcode area.

## Site

All operations run from a single site: Unit 12, Halden Industrial Park, Middleton, XX1 4LP. The site comprises a 2,400 square metre warehouse, a trade counter, and first-floor offices.

## What we sell

The catalogue covers approximately 3,100 lines across four ranges:

- **Cookware** - stainless steel pans, cast iron, non-stick
- **Cutlery and knives** - professional chef knives, blocks, sharpening equipment
- **Small appliances** - blenders, mixers, food processors
- **Front of house** - crockery, glassware, serving equipment

## Who we sell to

Roughly 70% of revenue comes from trade customers: independent restaurants, cafes, catering companies and small hotel groups, predominantly within a 60-mile radius of the site. The remaining 30% is direct-to-consumer through the online store.

## Structure

The company has six departments: Sales, Customer Services, Warehouse and Logistics, Finance, IT, and People. Each department head reports to the Managing Director. The senior team meets weekly on Monday mornings.

## Trading history

Turnover for the year ending 31 March 2026 was £8.2 million. The company has traded profitably every year since 2014.
