# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

All permanent full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year, in addition to the 8 English bank holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement calculated on contracted hours.

Employees with five or more years of continuous service receive one additional day per completed year, to a maximum of 30 days.

## Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

## Booking

Leave is requested through the People system at least two weeks before the intended start date for absences of up to five days, and at least six weeks before for longer absences. Requests are approved by the line manager.

Approval is not automatic. Managers must maintain adequate cover, and no more than two members of any single department may be on leave simultaneously. Requests are considered in the order received.

## Carry over

Up to five days may be carried into the following leave year. Carried days must be taken by 30 June or they are lost. Carry-over above five days requires written approval from the department head and is granted only where leave could not be taken for operational reasons.

## Bank holidays

Warehouse staff who are rostered to work on a bank holiday receive a day in lieu, to be taken within three months.

## Leaving the company

On termination, any accrued but untaken leave is paid in the final salary payment. Leave taken in excess of the accrued entitlement is deducted from final pay.
