# Order Processing Standard Operating Procedure

## Scope

This procedure covers trade and web orders from receipt to despatch. It does not cover returns, which are handled under OPS-02.

## 1. Order receipt

Orders arrive through the web store, by email to sales@, or by telephone. Telephone orders are entered into the order system by the sales advisor while the customer is on the line and read back for confirmation.

Every order receives a system-generated order number in the format NK-YYYYMMDD-NNNN.

## 2. Credit check

Trade orders are automatically held if the customer's account balance exceeds their credit limit or if any invoice is more than 60 days overdue. Held orders appear on the credit control queue and are released only by Finance under FIN-01.

## 3. Picking

Orders released before 14:00 are picked the same working day. The picker scans each item against the pick list. Any shortfall is recorded on the system immediately, not at the end of the pick.

Where stock is short, the order is despatched as a part shipment and the balance placed on back order, unless the customer's account is marked "no part shipment".

## 4. Checking

Every order is checked by a second operative against the pick list before packing. High-value orders above £750 are checked by a supervisor.

## 5. Packing and despatch

Knives and glassware are packed with edge protection and marked as fragile. The packing note is placed inside the parcel and the delivery label on the outside.

Orders confirmed before 14:00 are handed to the carrier the same day. Orders confirmed after 14:00 go the following working day.

## 6. Confirmation

The system emails despatch confirmation with tracking to the customer automatically on carrier collection.
