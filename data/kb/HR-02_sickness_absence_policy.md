# Sickness Absence Policy

## Reporting absence

An employee who is unable to attend work must telephone their line manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence, or before their shift start time if that is earlier. Text messages and emails are not acceptable for the first notification. Contact must be made by the employee personally unless they are physically unable to do so.

The employee must contact their manager again on day three and thereafter as agreed.

## Certification

| Absence length | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 to 7 calendar days | Self-certification form on return |
| 8 calendar days or more | Fit note from a GP, provided within 5 days |

## Sick pay

Statutory Sick Pay is payable from the fourth qualifying day of absence, subject to the statutory earnings threshold. The first three days are waiting days and are unpaid unless company sick pay applies.

Employees with more than 12 months of continuous service receive company sick pay at full salary for up to four weeks in any rolling 12-month period, inclusive of SSP. Company sick pay is discretionary and may be withheld where the absence procedure has not been followed.

## Return to work

A return-to-work discussion is held with the line manager on the first day back after every absence, however short.

## Trigger points

The following prompt a formal review under the absence management procedure:

- Three separate absences in a rolling six-month period
- Eight or more working days lost in a rolling six-month period
- Any pattern of absence adjoining weekends or annual leave
