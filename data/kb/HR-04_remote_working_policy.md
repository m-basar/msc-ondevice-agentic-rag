# Remote Working Policy

## Eligibility

Remote working is available to office-based roles where the duties can be performed effectively away from site. It is not available to warehouse, despatch, or trade counter roles, which are site-dependent by nature.

New employees work on site full time for the duration of their probationary period.

## Pattern

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to two days per week. Mondays are a core on-site day for all office staff because the senior team and departmental meetings are held that morning.

The pattern is agreed in writing with the line manager and reviewed every six months. It is not a contractual right and may be withdrawn where performance or business need requires it.

## Expectations

While working remotely, employees must:

- Be contactable during core hours of 09:30 to 16:00
- Have a working internet connection adequate for video calls
- Work from a location in the United Kingdom unless prior written approval has been given
- Store company data only in approved company systems, never on personal devices or personal cloud accounts

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides a laptop and headset. It does not reimburse home broadband, heating, or electricity. A second monitor for home use is provided to department heads only.

## Health and safety

Employees working remotely complete a display screen equipment self-assessment annually. The company remains responsible for the employee's health and safety while working, and any work-related injury at home must be reported under OPS-08.
