# IT Equipment Issue and Return

## Standard allocations

| Role | Equipment |
|---|---|
| Office-based staff | Laptop, docking station, two monitors, headset |
| Sales representatives | Laptop, mobile phone |
| Warehouse operatives | Shared handheld scanner, no personal device |
| Department heads | Laptop, mobile phone, second monitor for home use |

## Requesting equipment

Requests are raised through the IT helpdesk by the line manager, not by the individual. Requests for anything outside the standard allocation require written approval from the department head and, where the cost exceeds £750, from the Finance Manager.

Standard equipment is normally issued within five working days. Non-standard equipment depends on supplier lead time.

## Responsibilities

Equipment remains the property of Northgate Kitchenware Ltd. Staff are responsible for its reasonable care. Loss or theft must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours and, if the device held company data, this also triggers the security incident procedure in IT-03.

Personal use of company equipment is permitted within reason. Installing unlicensed software, disabling security software, or allowing family members to use a company device are not permitted.

## Returns

Equipment must be returned to the IT helpdesk on the final working day of employment or when changing to a role with a different allocation. IT wipes and re-images all returned devices before reissue. Devices more than four years old are securely disposed of through a certified recycling contractor, and a destruction certificate is retained.
