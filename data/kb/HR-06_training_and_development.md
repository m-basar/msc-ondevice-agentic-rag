# Training and Development

## Induction

All new employees complete a corporate induction on their first day, covering the company overview, health and safety, fire procedures, and data protection. Warehouse staff complete an additional half-day warehouse safety induction before working unsupervised.

## Mandatory training

| Training | Who | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Fire safety awareness | All staff | Annually |
| Data protection and information security | All staff | Annually |
| Manual handling | Warehouse and despatch | Every 2 years |
| Forklift truck operation | Nominated operators | Refresher every 3 years |
| First aid at work | Nominated first aiders | Every 3 years |
| Fire warden | Nominated wardens | Every 2 years |

Mandatory training is recorded on the People system. Completion is monitored by the People team, and overdue training is escalated to the department head monthly.

## Professional development

Each employee has an annual development conversation with their line manager in April, following the performance review cycle.

The company will consider funding professional qualifications relevant to the employee's role. Applications are made to the People team with a supporting business case from the line manager. Funding above £1,500 requires Managing Director approval.

Where the company funds a qualification costing more than £1,000, the employee signs a training agreement requiring repayment on a sliding scale if they leave within two years of completion.

## Study leave

Up to five days of paid study leave per year may be granted for company-funded qualifications, at the line manager's discretion.
