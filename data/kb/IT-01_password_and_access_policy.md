# Password and Access Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. The company follows NCSC guidance and encourages three random words rather than complex substitutions, which are harder to remember and no harder to crack.

Scheduled password rotation has been discontinued for standard accounts, in line with NCSC advice that forced rotation drives weaker password choices. Passwords are changed only where there is reason to believe an account is compromised.

Administrator accounts remain on a 180-day rotation.

Passwords must never be shared, written down where they can be found, or reused on personal accounts. The company provides a password manager and staff are expected to use it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all cloud services, including email, the order system, the finance system and the People system. Authenticator app or hardware token only; SMS codes are not accepted.

## Account provisioning

Accounts are created by IT on receipt of a starter form from the People team, and are granted the minimum access needed for the role. Additional access requires the data owner's written approval.

Access rights are reviewed every six months by each department head.

## Leavers

The People team notifies IT of every leaver at least five working days before the final working day, or immediately in the case of summary dismissal. Accounts are disabled at the end of the final working day, not deleted, and are retained in a disabled state for 90 days before removal.

## Shared and generic accounts

Shared accounts are not permitted except for the warehouse scanner accounts, which are tied to a device rather than a person. These have no access to personal data and cannot approve transactions.
