# Password and Access Policy

> This version has been withdrawn. See IT-11 for the policy in force.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 8 characters and must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character.

All passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system prevents reuse of the previous five passwords.

Passwords must never be shared, written down where they can be found, or reused on personal accounts.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required on remote access to email and the finance system. A code sent by SMS is acceptable where an authenticator app is not available.

Multi-factor authentication is not currently required on the order system or the People system when accessed from the office network.

## Account provisioning

Accounts are created by IT on receipt of a starter form from the People team, and are granted the minimum access needed for the role. Additional access requires the data owner's written approval.

Access rights are reviewed annually by each department head.

## Leavers

The People team notifies IT of every leaver at least five working days before the final working day, or immediately in the case of summary dismissal. Accounts are disabled at the end of the final working day, not deleted, and are retained in a disabled state for 90 days before removal.

## Shared and generic accounts

Shared accounts are not permitted except for the warehouse scanner accounts, which are tied to a device rather than a person. These have no access to personal data and cannot approve transactions.
