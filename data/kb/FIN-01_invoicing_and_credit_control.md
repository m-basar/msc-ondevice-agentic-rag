# Invoicing and Credit Control

## Invoicing

Invoices are raised automatically on despatch and emailed to the address held on the customer account. Paper invoices are issued only on request.

Standard payment terms are 30 days from the date of invoice. Terms are stated on the invoice and in the customer's account agreement.

## Credit accounts

New trade customers apply for a credit account using the account application form. Finance obtains a credit reference and takes two trade references before an account is opened. Opening limits are typically £2,500, reviewed after six months of trading.

Credit limit increases are approved by the Finance Manager up to £15,000, and by the Managing Director above that.

## Overdue accounts

| Days overdue | Action |
|---|---|
| 7 | Automated reminder email |
| 14 | Telephone call from credit control |
| 30 | Formal letter, account placed on hold |
| 60 | Final demand, referred to the Finance Manager |
| 90 | Referred to the debt collection agency |

An account on hold cannot place new orders until the balance is cleared. Order system holds are described in OPS-01.

## Disputes

A customer disputing an invoice must raise it within 14 days. Finance logs the dispute, and the disputed amount only is placed on hold. The undisputed balance remains payable on the original terms.

## Bad debt

Debts referred to the collection agency and unrecovered after six months are written off with Managing Director approval, and the customer account is closed to credit.
